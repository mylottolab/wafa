English

To delete your WAFA account: tap Request account deletion, log in with your WAFA nickname and password, then tap Delete account. In the app: WAFA → Settings (bottom) → Delete account at the very bottom. Or email weapps.kr@gmail.com with your WAFA nickname.

Deleted: account, chats, photos, videos, voice messages, call history and recordings, usage, settings and push tokens. Your WAFA number is unlinked and not reissued for 6 months. Deletion happens 7 days after the request (you can cancel before then).

Kept by law: payment and cancellation records for 5 years and dispute records for 3 years, without information that identifies you.

Store subscriptions are not cancelled automatically — cancel them in Google Play or App Store subscriptions.
